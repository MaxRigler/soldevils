Who We Are

More than a tailgate.
A shared commitment to ASU.

The Sol Devils began in 1999 with ten football fans tailgating around a few hibachis. What started as a small gathering of friends has grown into one of Arizona State’s established independent athletics booster organizations.

Our members are dedicated supporters of ASU Athletics. We take pride in the friendships we’ve built, the traditions we maintain, and the tangible support we provide for Sun Devil student-athletes.

What We Do

Making an impact beyond game day

Our members support ASU Athletics individually and come together when special projects need additional help. A few contributions from the club’s history:

2019 · Camp Tontozona

Sol Devil Field

$1 million field

Helped raise funds for a new artificial-turf practice field, preserving a cherished summer football tradition in the Arizona high country.

2016 · Athletic facilities

Student Athletic Facility

$25,000 funded

Funded a graphics project at the football program’s north-end-zone athletic facility.

2013 · Football operations

Football trams

$250,000 in 48 hours

Secured toward a $400,000 replacement of aging football transportation trams, which was fully funded within a month.

Membership

A close-knit club with a shared purpose

The Sol Devils are an independent booster organization with their own board, bylaws, and budget. Membership is intentionally limited to preserve the personal nature of the group.

Prospective members are introduced and sponsored by existing members, with applications considered through the club’s review process.

Read about membership (opens in a new tab)

Membership considerations

  • ASU football season ticket holder
  • Significant supporter of Sun Devil Athletics
  • Sponsored by two active Sol Devils
  • Approval through the club’s application process

Membership is capped at 90. Meeting the minimum criteria does not guarantee admission.

Game Day

The Sol Devils Tailgate

A football Saturday tradition and one of the club’s most recognizable activities. Members, invited guests, and sponsors gather outside the southwest entrance of Mountain America Stadium for food, drinks, music, and good company before kickoff.

Location
Southwest stadium entrance
When
Three hours before kickoff
Admission
Members, invited guests & sponsors
Typical crowd
600–1,000 guests, depending on the game
